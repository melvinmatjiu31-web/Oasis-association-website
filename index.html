Welcome to Oasis Association

Oasis Association is a non-profit organisation that creates opportunities for people with intellectual disabilities. The organisation provides support through developmental, residential, work and community-based programmes.

Oasis aims to help beneficiaries develop their abilities, participate in meaningful activities and become as independent and productive as possible.

Services & Projects

Oasis Association provides a range of programmes, services and projects that create meaningful opportunities for people with intellectual disabilities.

Recycling

Oasis operates recycling activities that provide practical work opportunities while supporting responsible environmental practices.

Bakery

The bakery is one of the income-generating projects associated with Oasis and provides meaningful work opportunities.

Second-Hand Shops

Oasis second-hand shops support the organisation's projects while providing useful goods to the community.

Books & Bric-a-Brac

Books and bric-a-brac activities form part of Oasis's range of community and income-generating projects.

Group Homes

Oasis provides group living opportunities as part of its support for people with intellectual disabilities.

Day Centres

Day centres provide activities and opportunities that support development, participation and community involvement.

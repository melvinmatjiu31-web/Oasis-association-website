About Oasis Association

Oasis Association is a non-profit organisation that creates opportunities for people with intellectual disabilities.

The organisation provides support, training, activities and meaningful opportunities that help people participate in their communities.

Oasis has developed a range of programmes and projects that support beneficiaries while also contributing to the wider community.

Our Mission

Oasis aims to create opportunities, encourage inclusion and support people with intellectual disabilities to live meaningful and productive lives.

What We Value

Our work is based on inclusion, dignity, opportunity and community participation.

Inclusion

Encouraging people to participate meaningfully in their communities.

Opportunity

Creating opportunities for learning, development and participation.

Community

Building stronger communities through participation and support.

Dignity

Supporting people with respect and recognising their abilities.

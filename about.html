About Oasis Association

Oasis Association is a non-profit organisation that creates opportunities for people with intellectual disabilities.

The organisation provides a variety of services and programmes that support personal development, meaningful activity, work opportunities, care and community participation.

Oasis aims to help beneficiaries realise their potential and become as independent and productive as possible.

Our History

The history of Oasis began with parents who wanted better educational opportunities for children with intellectual disabilities.

As the children grew older, their families recognised the need for opportunities beyond school. Oasis developed programmes that could provide meaningful activities, work opportunities, support and care throughout different stages of life.

Over the years, Oasis has developed a range of services and projects supporting children and adults with intellectual disabilities in communities across Cape Town.

Our Mission

Oasis's mission is to enable beneficiaries with intellectual disabilities to realise their fullest potential and become as independent and productive as possible.

Our Values

Dignity

We believe every person deserves to be treated with dignity, respect and compassion.

Inclusion

We support opportunities for people with intellectual disabilities to participate in their communities.

Independence

We encourage beneficiaries to develop their skills and achieve greater independence.

Community

We work with families, businesses, volunteers and communities to create opportunities.

Who We Support

Oasis provides support to children and adults with intellectual disabilities. Services are designed according to the different needs of beneficiaries.

  • Children requiring specialised developmental support.
  • Adults participating in meaningful work activities.
  • Adults requiring supported living arrangements.
  • Adults who require supervised activity programmes.
  • Families and caregivers who need additional support.

Help Us Create More Opportunities
